# Alexander Dunn — Analytic and Arithmetic Applications of Half Integral Weight Automorphic Forms

**Year:** 2020  
**Source:** [thesis or companion publication](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/115703/bitstreams/378457/data.pdf)

The dissertation separates twisted moments of half-integral-weight L-series, Ramanujan's third-order mock theta function, and half-integral-weight Kloosterman sums. Do not attach an Euler product to its half-integral-weight L-series: Dunn explicitly says it lacks one in general.
